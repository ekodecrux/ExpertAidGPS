Privacy Policy for Expertaid GPS Tracking

1. Introduction

   1.1 Welcome to Expertaid GPS Tracking. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our GPS Tracking platform for school buses, employee transportation, and fleet management.

2. Information We Collect

   2.1 Personal Information
We may collect information including your name, mobile number, email address, organization name, employee ID, student details, and login credentials when you register or use our services.

   2.2 Location Information
We collect GPS location data from registered vehicles and authorized mobile devices to provide real-time tracking, route monitoring, and location-based services.

   2.3 Device Information
We may collect information about your mobile device such as device model, operating system, IP address, app version, and unique device identifiers for security and performance improvements.

3. How We Use Your Information

   3.1 We use GPS location data to provide:
• Real-time vehicle tracking
• Student & Employee pickup monitoring
• Route history
• ETA calculations
• Geo-fencing alerts
• Emergency notifications

   3.2 Personal information is used for:
• User authentication
• Customer support
• Service updates
• Account management
• Improving application performance

4. Sharing of Information

   4.1 We do not sell or rent your personal information.

   4.2 Information may be shared with authorized schools, organizations, transport administrators, or fleet managers for operational purposes only.

   4.3 Information may also be disclosed if required by applicable laws or government authorities.

5. Location Permissions

   5.1 The application requires location access to provide accurate GPS tracking and route monitoring services.

   5.2 Location access can be managed through your mobile device settings. Disabling location services may affect certain application features.

6. Data Security

   6.1 We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, misuse, disclosure, or loss.

   6.2 Although we strive to protect your information, no electronic transmission or storage system is completely secure.

7. Data Retention

   7.1 We retain personal information and GPS tracking records only as long as necessary to provide our services or comply with legal obligations.

8. Children's Privacy

   8.1 Our platform is intended for use by schools, parents, organizations, and authorized administrators. Student information is collected only with authorization from the respective institution or guardian.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

   9.1 We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published within the application or on our official website.
